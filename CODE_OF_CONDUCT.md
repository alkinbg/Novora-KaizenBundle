# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want Novora KaizenBundle to be a respectful, constructive, and welcoming project.

Positive participation includes:

- Respecting different backgrounds, viewpoints, and experience levels.
- Giving technical feedback based on evidence and without personal attacks.
- Assuming good faith, acknowledging mistakes, and staying open to corrections.
- Keeping discussions focused on problems and practical improvements.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or insults.
- Personal attacks and deliberate disruption of technical discussions.
- Publishing anyone's private information without permission.
- Any behavior that would reasonably make participants feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project community spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may moderate, edit, remove, or reject comments, issues, contributions, or interactions that violate this code. Repeated or serious misconduct may lead to temporary or permanent participation restrictions.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to **alkinbg@gmail.com**. Please include enough context to investigate without forwarding sensitive personal information unnecessarily. Reports will be handled as privately and fairly as reasonably possible.
